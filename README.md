# Project taken down

Out of respect for the legacy and future work of Argonaut Games, I have decided to take down this project.

This decision was not the result of legal intervention, and no frustration should be directed toward Argonaut in any way.

Thank you all for your overwhelming support and feedback. It is wonderful to see how beloved this game remains after so many years. Please direct that love and support toward Argonaut and all their future projects.
